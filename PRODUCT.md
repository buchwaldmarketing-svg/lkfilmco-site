# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Engaged couples planning Dallas/Fort Worth weddings (plus some destination), mostly finding Lee through Instagram, referrals and Google, usually browsing on a phone. They are comparing wedding filmmakers at the $3,000 to $6,500 level and deciding whether Lee's films feel like their day. Now booking 2027.

## Product Purpose
lkfilmco.com is Lee Kounas's own wedding film business site (LK Film Co, filming since 2020). Its job is bookings: show the films, make the collections and prices obvious early, and make checking a date effortless (contact form with the collection preselected, or text 214-577-4801). Success = more 2027 inquiries at the current price points with less back-and-forth.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, warm, personable. Lee is natural, funny and easy to be around on the day ("nothing posed, nothing missed"). Copy is confident, minimal and editorial, in Lee's own first-person voice. Never stiff, never salesy.

## Anti-references
- Wedding clichés in copy: "love story", "timeless memories", "special day".
- Gold accents (retired), olive green (Mark Keysor's palette, a direct competitor), neon, marquees, ghost letters, carousels/sliders.
- SaaS pricing tables, checkmarks, feature grids; technical inclusions (color grading, licensed music, lighting, two-camera setup).
- Soft/romantic template looks (arches, script fonts, pill buttons).
- Concept gimmicks (timecodes, film-library numbering, movie-poster billing blocks).
- Mentioning planners as partners (none are current).

## Design Principles
1. The footage leads: real films and loops from Lee's latest weddings carry the luxury.
2. Easy to book: prices early, one tap from a collection to a preselected inquiry, text-first option.
3. Keep what Lee loves: the hero treatment and the menu are his; refine around them.
4. Editorial restraint: espresso, light neutrals, Cormorant serif with italic accents; luxury through spacing and type, not decoration.
5. His words, real facts: real prices, real reviews only (two exist), nothing invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Text over video needs shading. Respect reduced motion. 44px tap targets on phones. Mobile (375px) is the priority viewport.
