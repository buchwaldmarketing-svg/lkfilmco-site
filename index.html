Marketing grad from UT Dallas, self-taught filmmaker, husband, dad of two.

I started filming my own kids and realized video holds something a photo can't: the voices, the movement, the way a moment actually felt. That's what pulled me into weddings.

Couples say working with me feels natural. And fun. I'm not a fly on the wall. I'm personable, I'll make you laugh, and I know how to get what I need without making it weird. Based in Dallas, powered by espresso over ice.
