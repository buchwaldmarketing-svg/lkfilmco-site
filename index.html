The experience

From helloto your finished film

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Send me your date, venue, and anything you want me to know about your day. I'll get back to you within a few hours.

  2. 02

    Let's connect

    We'll chat over text or email, whatever's easiest. Your day, your vibe, what matters most to you. No sales pitch, just a real conversation.

  3. 03

    Pre-wedding planning

    About a month out we go over your timeline together, and I coordinate with your planner and photographer so the day runs smooth.

  4. 04

    Your wedding day

    I show up early, crack a joke to break the tension, and capture everything: the first look nerves, the ugly-cry toasts, the dance floor chaos.

  5. 05

    Your film

    Built from scratch in the edit, never a template. Delivered to a private gallery to stream, download, and share. Yours forever.
