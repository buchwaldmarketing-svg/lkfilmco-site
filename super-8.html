How It Works

Real film feel, none of the risk.

01

Filmed like Super 8

On your wedding day I shoot the way a film camera would force me to. Small moments, simple movement, nothing over-produced.

02

Digital underneath

Real Super 8 stock is fragile, expensive, and silent. Filming digitally means your vows and toasts still have sound, and nothing gets lost at a lab.

03

The look, dialed in

The film style goes on in the edit, and I control how strong it gets. A subtle hint of texture or the full 1970s home movie. We set it where it feels right for you.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I add the Super 8 look to any package?

Yes. It layers into any package, whether you want a few nostalgic moments or a full Super 8 style wedding video. Mention it when you inquire and we will build it into your day.

Will the whole film look like Super 8?

Only if you want it to. Some couples like a handful of grainy moments tucked inside a clean, modern film. Others want the entire thing to feel like found family footage. You choose, and I can set the strength anywhere in between.

Where does the look show up best?

Getting ready, golden hour, the dance floor, the exit. The moments that already feel nostalgic are the ones that shine with the film treatment.
